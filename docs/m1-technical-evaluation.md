> **Synthetic personas only.** This checks that the engine behaves as designed. It is *not* evidence that real people
> prefer adaptive discovery (hypothesis H-02 remains unvalidated until real-use evaluation).

**Top-5 hit** = sessions whose shortlist contains one of the persona's 5 best-matching dishes. 
**Mean shortlist match** = persona's hidden taste score of shortlisted dishes (−1..1). 
**Variety** = distinct dish structures among the 10 cards.
